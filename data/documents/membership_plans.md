# Membership Plans

There are three tiers: **Free**, **Plus**, and **Pro**.

## Pricing

- Free: $0.
- Plus: **$9.99 per month**.
- Pro: **$19.99 per month**.

Choosing annual billing gives you **2 months free** compared to paying monthly.

## What each tier includes

- **Plus**: free standard shipping on every order, and early access to sales.
- **Pro**: everything in Plus, plus **5% cashback** on purchases and priority customer
  support.

## Trials and discounts

Pro includes a **14-day free trial**. A payment card is required to start the trial,
and it is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel first.

Students get **50% off the Plus plan** after verifying their student status.

## Cancellation

You can cancel at any time. Your membership benefits remain active until the end of
the current billing period. There are no cancellation fees.
