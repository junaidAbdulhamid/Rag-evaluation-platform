# Warranty Policy

Every product includes a **limited warranty of 12 months** from the date of purchase.

## What is covered

- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
- Rechargeable batteries are covered for the first **6 months** only.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, drops, or liquid/water damage.
- Normal cosmetic wear and tear.
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications.

## Extended warranty

An extended warranty adding **24 months** of coverage can be purchased for **$39.99**
within 30 days of the original purchase.

## Making a claim

A valid proof of purchase is required for every claim. When a claim is approved, we
will either repair or replace the product at our discretion. Replacement units may be
new or professionally refurbished.
