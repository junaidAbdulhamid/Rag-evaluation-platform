# Refund Policy

Customers have **30 days from the delivery date** to request a refund. Requests made
after 30 days will not be accepted.

## Eligibility

- Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging.
- Digital goods are non-refundable once they have been downloaded.
- Opened electronics are subject to a 15% restocking fee.

## Processing

Approved refunds are processed within **5 to 7 business days**. Refunds are always
issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. We cannot refund to a
different card or account.

## How to request

Start a refund from the online returns portal, or email `support@example.com` with
your order number. Shipping charges are only refunded if the return is the result of
our error (for example, a wrong or defective item was sent).
