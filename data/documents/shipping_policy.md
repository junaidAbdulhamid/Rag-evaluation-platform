# Shipping Policy

We currently ship to the **United States and Canada only**.

## Domestic (United States)

| Method    | Delivery time        | Cost                        |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------------------|
| Standard  | 3-5 business days     | $4.99 (free over $50)       |
| Express   | 1-2 business days     | $14.99                      |
| Overnight | Next business day     | $29.99 (order by 2:00pm ET) |

## Canada

Delivery takes **7 to 10 business days**. Customers are responsible for any customs
duties or import fees charged on delivery.

## Processing

Orders are processed Monday through Friday. Orders placed on weekends or public
holidays begin processing the next business day. A tracking number is emailed within
**24 hours** of the order shipping.
