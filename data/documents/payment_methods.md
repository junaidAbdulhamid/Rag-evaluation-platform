# Payment Methods

All prices and charges are in **US dollars (USD)**.

## Accepted methods

- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover.
- Digital wallets: PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay.

We do not accept paper checks, money orders, or bank wire transfers.

## When you are charged

Your card is charged **when the order ships**, not when the order is placed. For
orders that ship in multiple packages, each shipment is charged separately.

## Installments

Orders over **$100** are eligible for installments through Affirm, split into **4
interest-free payments**. Approval is subject to a credit check by Affirm.

## Gift cards

Gift cards never expire. They can be applied to any order but cannot be redeemed for
cash except where required by law.
