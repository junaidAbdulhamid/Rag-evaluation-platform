# Account Security

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least **12 characters** long and contain at least one number and
one symbol. Password reset links are valid for **1 hour** after they are sent.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is optional and can be enabled with an authenticator
app or by SMS. We strongly recommend the authenticator app option.

## Lockouts and sessions

After **5 failed sign-in attempts**, the account is locked for **30 minutes**. Signed-in
sessions expire after **14 days** of inactivity and require a fresh sign-in.

## Staying safe

We will never ask for your password by email or phone. If you receive a message
asking for it, report it to `security@example.com`.
